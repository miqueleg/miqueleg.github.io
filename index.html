Research

Five scales, one enzyme

Quantum mechanics for the electrons, molecular mechanics for the protein, QM/MM to join them, and the methods I develop at each level. The ruler on the left tracks length and time; the panels on the right run live in your browser. Panels marked real data play my own calculations; the others are schematics of the method.

  1. QM1 Åfs10–100 atoms

    Electrons

    DFT · xTB / g-xTB · ML potentials (AIQM2, MACE, UMA)

    Where are the electrons, and where do they go when a bond breaks?

    Semi-empirical and machine-learned potentials make it cheap enough to scan, optimise and search for transition states on hundreds of atoms, then check the key points with DFT.

    Methods I builtgxtb-pymol-interfaceMLatom-Sella-TS-search

  2. QM10 Åfs–ps100–400 atoms

    Active site

    QM cluster models · relaxed scans · TS search (Sella) · EVB

    What does the reaction look like at the atomic level, and what does it cost?

    A 303-atom cluster model of a halohydrin dehalogenase active site, computed with AIQM2 through ORCA. Cyanide is pulled towards the epoxide carbon one step at a time. At the last step the epoxide C–O bond breaks and the catalytic tyrosine hands its proton to the leaving oxygen, while the arginine re-protonates the tyrosine.

    Methods I builtMLatom-ORCA-interfacehow the scan was run

  3. QM/MM30 Åps10⁴ atoms

    Protein environment

    QM/MM electrostatic embedding · environment-aware RESP

    How does the enzyme polarise the substrate before anything reacts?

    Charges fitted in the gas phase miss the field of the protein. Fitting them inside the enzyme, with the protein as point charges, gives ligand parameters that match where the ligand actually sits.

    Methods I builtQMMESPprotonation-optimizer

  4. MM100 Åns–µs10⁵ atoms

    Dynamics

    MD (OpenMM, Amber) · enhanced sampling · MSMs · tunnel analysis

    Which conformations does the enzyme visit, and which ones are active?

    Long simulations, projected onto a few collective variables, show which states an enzyme samples and how mutations shift the balance, such as the flexible N-terminal loop that controls HheG's activity and enantioselectivity.

    Methods I builtMDTunnel

    PaperHheG loop

  5. Design20Nsequence spaceN = 254 residues

    Design

    AlphaFold ensembles · LigandMPNN · BindCraft · Boltz-2

    Which positions should we change, and to what?

    Simulations point to the residues that matter; design tools and the lab test what to put there. In HheC, the single change F12Y in sequence motif 1 raised specific activity up to 5-fold and the apparent melting temperature by 10 K.

    PapersHheC motif 1AlphaFold2 ensembles

Method development

Methods I build for QM, QM/MM and MM

The simulations above run on methods I wrote when the existing ones fell short: QM workflows with machine-learned potentials, QM/MM charge derivation and MM set-up. All are open source.

QM → MM

protonation-optimizer

★ 20

Picks histidine tautomers (HID/HIE/HIP) from PropKa3 pKa values plus xTB energies of each local environment, before MD.

Python · xTB · PropKa3 · MDTraj · blog post

QM/MM

QMMESP

★ 8

RESP charges for ligands computed inside the protein with electrostatically embedded QM/MM, written straight into Amber prepi files.

Python · PySCF / ORCA · Multiwfn · blog post

QM

MLatom-ORCA-interface

Couples the AIQM2 potential in MLatom with ORCA's optimiser and relaxed scans, for cluster-model calculations.

MLatom · ORCA · AIQM2 · blog post

QM

MLatom-Sella-TS-search

Transition-state searches with MLatom (AIQM2) as the calculator and Sella as the optimiser.

MLatom · Sella · ASE · blog post

QM

gxtb-pymol-interface

A PyMOL plugin that runs xTB and g-xTB: optimisations, frequencies, NEB paths and IRC-like relaxations from the viewer.

PyMOL · Qt · xTB / g-xTB

MM

MDTunnel

Moves a ligand along a protein tunnel one sphere at a time, minimising at each step, and returns an energy profile.

OpenMM · OpenFF · AutoDock Vina
